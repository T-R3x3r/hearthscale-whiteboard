# Whiteboard

A whiteboard for sketches and diagrams in a Hearthscale tab, built on the open-source [Excalidraw](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw) editor. Your boards stay in the app on this computer, and Whiteboard never goes on the network. Your apps' agents can read a board, draw shapes, labels and arrows on it, and look at it with you.

## Get started

Open Whiteboard from the rail and draw. The menu at the top left makes a new board, and **Boards** lists them all, where you open, rename or delete one. Paste or drop a picture on the board, or pick one with the picture tool. Everything you draw is kept as you draw it, with no limit on the size of a board.

To have an agent help, ask it in a conversation:

> Draw a diagram of my morning routine on the whiteboard: wake up, make coffee, walk the dog, start work.

The agent draws on the board you opened last, and brings the board to the front beside the conversation. You can move, change or undo whatever it draws, as you can your own work. The board follows Hearthscale's light or dark look.

## What Whiteboard asks for

- **Its own data on this computer.** Your boards, their pictures and your library of shapes are kept in the app's own storage.
- **Writes to your clipboard.** **Copy to clipboard as PNG** puts a picture of what you selected on your clipboard.
- **Updates from its own work.** When an agent draws, the board on your screen shows it at once.
- **Tools for your apps' agents.** An agent reads the board, draws on it and looks at it as a picture. Drawing changes your board.

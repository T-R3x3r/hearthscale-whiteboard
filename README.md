# Whiteboard

A whiteboard for sketches and diagrams in a Hearthscale tab. The board is the [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) website, and your apps' agents can read it, draw shapes, labels and arrows on it, and look at it with you. You need no account.

## Get started

Open Whiteboard from the rail and draw. To have an agent help, ask it in a conversation:

> Draw a diagram of my morning routine on the whiteboard: wake up, make coffee, walk the dog, start work.

You can move, change or undo whatever an agent draws, as you can your own work. The board follows Hearthscale's light or dark look.

## What Whiteboard asks for

- **excalidraw.com.** The board loads from excalidraw.com, and its fonts from Excalidraw's file host. Every other address is refused, so Excalidraw's sharing by link and live collaboration do not work here.
- **Tools for your apps' agents.** An agent reads the board, draws on it and looks at it in the page, which is on the network.
- **The page's storage.** Excalidraw keeps your board in the page's own storage on this computer, not in an account.
